### Topics with no workbook session

Moore and Mealy machines ([topics 6](topic-06.html) and [7](topic-07.html)), PDA acceptance ([topic 15](topic-15.html)), context-sensitive grammars ([topic 19](topic-19.html)), linear bounded automata ([topic 20](topic-20.html)) and TMs as acceptors ([topic 23](topic-23.html)) are in the syllabus but have no workbook session. Their topic pages have their own tutorial problems.

### How to use the problems

1. Attempt each problem on paper, in full, before looking at any notes.
2. Check your answer against the **Key ideas** and the **Worked example** on the topic page.
3. Take the topic quiz. If you score below 60%, go back over the problems you found hardest.
